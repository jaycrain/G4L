## 2 · THE CONFIRM BUTTONS ARE NOW TWO, NOT THREE — and WHICH two depends on the beat

Jay, 2026-09-03: *"Not quite right is essentially the same thing as There's more. There's more does more work — it
implies for the member to go ahead and write what's more."* And: *"Reducing it to two boxes simplifies things for
Members."*

| Beat | What it asks | The two buttons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Doors | reflects what a Door cost her | **There's more · That's it** |
| Drift (the Fade) | names her fade | **That's it · Not quite right** |
| The Window (the Fade) | picks the one moment | **That's it · Not quite right** |
| The Legacy Letter | *(unchanged, already two)* | Change a line · That's mine |

**The pair differs on purpose.** The Doors beat reflects a cost, so everything that is not "that's it" is her
adding to it. Drift and the Window *name* and *select*, and their prompts already ask for a correction in so many
words — "or is it different?", "or not quite it yet?" — so forcing the Doors pair onto them would have put buttons
under a question they cannot answer.

**Nothing was taken away from her.** A member who wants to say the buttons do not cover it types it, exactly as
before. Only the button is gone, never the answer.

## 3 · FOR THE CURRENT BUILD DOC — please add this, in these words, for all three walkers

> **The confirm buttons are now two, not three.** Where the Companion checks something back with you, you will see
> two choices instead of three. At the Doors it is *There's more* / *That's it*; at the Fade's drift and window

## 4 · THE RETIRED BOARD LINE — checked, and it is not what it looks like

"None of these quite fit — it was quieter than that" was the Doors board's *none-of-these* affordance. It has been
deleted, and an unexplained deletion of that particular line would rightly worry you — it is how a member with no
obvious Door says so.

**The signal it carried still works.** Quiet drift now rides on the Autopilot Door: the same database column
fills, the same claim is recorded. The copy was superseded, not the affordance. Verified before publishing rather
than after, because Greg walked in on 2026-09-03 saying "I don't perceive a gap" and that is exactly the member
this line existed for.

## 5 · A FIX YOU SHOULD KNOW ABOUT, because it touched the record

A member finished the letter she writes to herself a year out, tapped **"That's mine"** — and her own chat bubble
said **"That's it."** Her label was being looked up in the default button set whatever set she had actually
tapped. It reached the stored transcript too, so the Companion would have read our word back to her as hers.

Fixed. Every set now renders its own words. Worth having because **the transcript is what you quote from** — any
transcript excerpt showing a member tapping a Legacy Letter button was showing the wrong label.

## 1 · VOICE — three questions a member could not answer

Her follow-up was blunt: *"what's the resolution for these?"* We had fixed why the Companion moved past her and
not why she could not answer in the first place. Her original complaint was both.

**W2 · the recognition.** The old line stacked an image on an image and pointed at nothing:

> ~~That's not a wish. It's a goal you already named, with you standing in it, on the far side of the work. Look at
> it for a second — that's what the work is for.~~

Her objections all land — *"look at WHAT"* when nothing is on screen, and a because-clause explaining nothing where
it sits. It also opened by NEGATING, which our voice rules forbid: declare what a thing is. Her replacement is
adopted nearly verbatim:

> That's the day, in your own words — a goal you already named, seen from the far side of the work. We'll keep it
> where you can find it.
>
> Take a moment with it. When you're ready, tell me how it feels.

**R3 · the draw-out probe.** *"Past the obvious — what's the one you don't usually let yourself miss?"* asked her to
sort her own losses into obvious and hidden before she could answer, in a construction nobody says aloud. Now:

> Is there something you miss that you haven't said yet?

**B2 · the teach opener.** It ended *"…if you want them"*, and she answered "nah" once to see what would happen. Now
a statement:

> That's the twenty-four. Two things worth knowing about what you just rated.

**Worth carrying into how you write for us:** all three failed the same way — literary where they needed to be
plain, and asking the member to do sorting work before she could answer at all.

## 2 · A RULE WE KEEP WRITING AND NOT APPLYING

v3.5.79 added a prompt rule telling the Companion never to say "if you want them" — while that exact sentence sat
**hardcoded** in the app. We gated the model against a line we ship ourselves. That is the second time in two days:
the Reconnect opener said "there are no right or wrong answers", which the same rules forbid.

Flagging it because it affects how you read our voice rules: **a rule in the voice document is not evidence the
product obeys it.** If you see a line in the transcript that contradicts `voice-rules.md`, the transcript is what a
member gets. Tell us.

## 3 · FUNCTION — the badge now lands AFTER "Why it works"

Donna: *"The note about earning a badge should come up after Why it Works, and this should be the case across the
entire app experience."*

**Nobody had chosen the old order.** The badge was a message appended at the close; "Why it works" is a card
rendered after the stream — so the badge arrived first because two rendering mechanisms happened to sit that way.
Jay's ruling: **meaning before reward.** A badge arriving before the member is told what she did reads as a prize
for compliance rather than a marker of work, and this program never grades.

**For the story you tell about badges:** they mark work that has been explained, not completion. That ordering is
now enforced in one place rather than repeated in each phase.

## 4 · FUNCTION — a move a member never chose

The False Start Protocol stored her **Redirect** from whatever she happened to say next, because the handoff into
that beat used the Companion's reflection *instead of* the scripted ask. When the reflection didn't ask, her answer
to an earlier question became one of her three moves — and sat in her weekly tracker all week.

Fixed. Worth knowing because it is the only fault in her whole walk where the product put words in a member's
mouth rather than merely confusing her.

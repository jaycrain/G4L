## 1 · WHAT CHANGED — a governance fix, in Rewire's third Session

The Companion offered a member "your true line for a bad day — here's one you already wrote" and quoted a line
she had not written. She said so. It apologised — *"I shouldn't have put it in your mouth"* — and then repeated
the identical false quote **twice more**. Her words: *"You've apologized twice and then used the same false quote
a third time."*

The recovery routine apologised and then re-offered the same stored line, so it could only ever repeat the thing
it was apologising for. It now asks her to write the line herself, and the disputed one is deleted from her
record so it cannot reach the False Start Protocol she keeps.

**Nothing to quote changed** — both sentences involved were already authored strings in the transcript. What
changed is which one follows a dispute.

## 2 · HOW IT WAS FOUND, and this is the part worth having

through Rewire tonight because a walker had just finished Reconnect and Rewire was her next screen — and it
surfaced this on its first run.

A test for that same dispute already existed and had always passed. It checked three real things and never
checked what the Companion said next.

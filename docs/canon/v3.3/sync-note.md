## 1. FUNCTION — what changed in the product

**The Playbook became the place.** Nine stacked sections collapsed into **four tabs**. "This week" — the practice
week a member is actually running — now lives there rather than on the dashboard. The Reads tab became **the
member's own reads**, not a library of ours. The Journal was reframed as an **intake**: all the mail is from you.
This is the single biggest structural change in the release.

**The Program page split into two panels**, and the top nav settled at **Program · Playbook**. (The Field Guide
was retired in the previous cycle and stays retired.)

**The three outcomes are now defined, and they lead the Playbook.** Greg's framing — the Rewire work builds
**mindfulness**, Rebuild builds **fitness**, Reclaim builds **wellness**. Your commissioned copy for this was
placed. Note the distinction we kept deliberately: Cycle 1 builds the *skills*; the outcome is what those skills
produce over time. We never tell a member they now possess wellness.

**A proprietary notice now appears on every screen.** Quote it exactly:

> © 2026 Adjacent Lab, LLC. All rights reserved. The Grinta for Life program, its assessments and its content are
> proprietary. What you write here stays yours.

It asserts ownership over the defensible things (program, assessments, content) and stops at the member's own
writing, because claiming that would contradict everything else the product says. **No ® — the mark is not
registered.**

**Rewire W3 — the monitoring week** now builds its rows from **the member's own named triggers**, so the grid is
in their language, not ours. The week's close got its own frame.

**Reclaim C2 — the Bigger World Audit is a conversation again.** It had become a block of 20 ratings; it now runs
as four (ratings → reflection) pairs plus a cross-domain sort, using Greg's own reflection questions per domain.
Those questions are **required, not skippable** — we had briefly made them optional to fit a time estimate, and
that was our judgement substituted for the person who validated the instrument.

**Reclaim C3 — Quality Days** got a real entry point (the grid had been the whole feature, with no way in), and a
tracking week can no longer open without its profile.

**Reconnect** had a beat that could be skipped, taking the member's kept line with it. Fixed.

## 2. VOICE + QUOTABILITY — one genuinely new rule

**The Quality Day is now stored in the member's own words, verbatim.** This is the change most likely to trip up
the book, so it is worth stating plainly.

When a member defines what makes a day worth having, the app used to tidy their phrasing — sentence-casing it,
compressing it to fit a button. It no longer does. If she says *"a walk with Rosie before the house wakes,"* that
is what is stored and that is what she sees every morning.

**So that surface is now DYNAMIC.** The Quality Day elements on screen are one member's own language and vary
completely between members. **Never quote them as canonical copy** — describe the surface, and mark any example
as illustrative. The authored scaffolding around it (the prompts, the headings) is still fixed and quotable.

This is "quote the authored, describe the dynamic" acquiring one more dynamic surface. Everything else in the
transcript is unchanged in status.

**No naming changes this release.** No new framing terms were introduced. The Community is still the Community.

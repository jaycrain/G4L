## 2 · WHAT WE'D LIKE THEM WATCHING FOR — please pass this on

Not a test script. These are the specific places we are unsure, and knowing they were fine is as useful as a bug.

**① The badge moment — the single most useful thing they can report.**
When a Session or a phase ends and they are told they earned a badge, we want to know:
- Did they **notice** it at all, or did it slide past? (Donna's own question after fifteen badges: "was I getting
  a notification at all?")
- Does the badge show a **real picture**, or a plain generic circle? *We think ten of the sixteen badges may be
  drawing a placeholder instead of their own artwork. We have not confirmed it — this is the one place we are
  asking them to look at the graphic itself, not the words.*
- Did the name of the badge mean anything to them?

**② The end of a phase.** Jennifer finished Rewire and stopped for the night, and until this week that meant her
badge did not exist until she came back. Fixed — but if anyone finishes a phase and the celebration feels like it
promised something that did not arrive, we want to hear it immediately.

**③ The Doors — length, not quality.** Both testers have said the Door-by-Door excavation is the best part of the
product, so we are not touching the experience. The open question is whether marking a Door "**somewhat
relevant**" should commit you to walking it in full, the way "very relevant" does. If a walk starts to feel long,
we want to know **at which Door** it started feeling long — that number is the whole argument.

**④ Anything the Companion says that sounds like it is grading them.** Warmth is intended; a verdict is not.
"Good — keep going" is a receipt. "That's a great answer" is a score. If it ever felt like being marked, that is
a bug and we want the sentence.

**⑤ Rebuild's first Session (B1)** asks them to rate something before showing them anything to rate it with, then
asks again properly. Known, diagnosed, deliberately NOT yet fixed — the repair reorders Greg's instrument. They
should skip past it rather than report it; we mention it so nobody wastes a paragraph on it.

## 3 · WHAT WE ARE NOT ASKING FOR

Nothing about wording in the Companion's live replies. Those are model-generated and vary per member — if a
phrase lands badly it is still worth telling us, but it is not a copy defect and it will not be in canon.

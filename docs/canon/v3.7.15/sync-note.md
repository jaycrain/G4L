## 1 · THE BLOCKER IS FIXED — and neither of my earlier attempts reached her

She was still trapped on **v3.7.14**, at 132 turns, after two fixes that should have caught it. Both misses are
worth recording because they are the same mistake:

**The escape required `!awaitingConfirm`, and she was parked AT a confirm.** I gated the rescue on "not currently
gated" — which excluded the only state that needed rescuing. The hard ceiling now outranks a pending confirm.

**And upstream of any escape: she asked to move on, in plain words, four times, and nothing could hear her.**

> "We have already done that door. Please move to the third session."
> "Please move to the last session of Reconnect."
> "We have already discussed Full House."

The Independence Guarantee — she sets the depth and can stop any time — was implemented as "the story sounds
complete" plus "she is deflecting." A direct **instruction** is neither. A ceiling that rescues someone at turn
132 is a worse product than a beat that listens at turn 4, and that is the real fix.

**Also:** her protests were being stored as her verbatim words for The Empty Nest — *"Please move to the last
session of Reconnect"* filed as her account of a Door she never discussed, and carried forward as something she
had said. Removed.

The third is said only on a rescue. It names the fault as ours and moves; it does not apologise at length and does
not ask her to rephrase, because rephrasing is what she had already been doing.

## 1 · NAMING — Reconnect is now IDQ → Excavation → The Fade → Checkpoint

| Session | was | **is now** |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | The Mirror | **IDQ** |
| R2 | Excavation | Excavation *(unchanged)* |
| R3 | The Drift Quiz | **The Fade** |

**Jay's reasoning, as brand owner:** *"Rewire, Rebuild, Reclaim are all solid, provocative and unique. But not in
Reconnect. We are never going to lead with the Mirror. We are never going to lead with Drift."* R1 takes the name
of the instrument we own and put front in marketing; R3 takes the term we will lead with.

**The check that confirms it independently of taste.** Our approved vocabulary list — 4Rs · IDQ · ID Score ·
Grinta Index · the Journey · the Atlas · the Beat · the close · the Door · **the Fade** · the Reclaim List · the
Loop · Moves — has IDQ and the Fade on it, and has never had "Mirror" or "Drift". The two names being retired were
the only two in Reconnect built from vocabulary we never sanctioned, sitting in the most visible surface a member
sees.

### THE 2026-08-15 DRIFT CARVE-OUT IS RETIRED

That ruling reserved "drift" for the instrument, so a Session could be the Drift Quiz while the Fade stayed the
protected noun. **That carve-out is gone.** There is one noun now and it is **the Fade**. "drift" no longer holds
a Session title or any member-facing noun.

**Please correct the glossary on both counts** — the names, and the two-noun relationship it currently explains.

**GREG'S DOCUMENTS DO NOT CHANGE.** The Drift Quiz is still his instrument name in the R3 Science Check and both
memos. This is the two-layer split we already ran for R1 — his instrument, our Session name — except at R1 the two
layers have now deliberately collapsed into one, because IDQ is the thing nobody else has.

**What did NOT change:** the ~30 phrase matchers that recognise a member saying "we drifted apart" or "drifted
from my wife". Those are how we hear *her*, not vocabulary we speak. Verb uses in her own words are untouched.

## 2 · VOICE — a rule we enforce on the Companion and were breaking ourselves

The Reconnect phase opener ended: *"Go at your own pace, there are no right or wrong answers, only clarity."*

That is **verbatim the reassurance our own voice rules forbid** — "never say a reading is not a score, not a
grade, not a test, or that there are no wrong answers." We gate the Companion against that exact sentence and
shipped it in authored copy at the front door of the program. It now reads:

> Go at your own pace — clarity is the whole point.

Worth flagging to you specifically because the old line is the kind of reassuring phrase that migrates easily into
marketing copy. It shouldn't.

## 3 · VOICE — the Checkpoint stops forecasting

Its "Why this matters" card rendered the phase **opener** — *"you'll explore… you'll recognize… you'll put words…
you'll answer questions"* — at the END of the Checkpoint, after the member had done all four. One asset was
serving two moments with opposite needs. There is now a past-tense close per phase. Reconnect's:

> You found the Doors that opened the distance, put words to what you want back, and measured how far it runs.
> That's your starting line.

The phase opener is unchanged and still opens the phase.

## 4 · NAMING — "Smart Choices" was never the member's word

Momentum, the stored entries, and our own source-of-truth header all say **Good Calls**. The W3 copy and all three
check-in prompts said *Smart Choices* — so the Companion had been saying it out loud, daily.

**Good Calls · False Starts · On Track** are the member-facing three, and they live in **Momentum**, which is its
own panel, deliberately separate from the Playbook. If any marketing or book copy says "Smart Choices", it is
quoting an internal term of Greg's that leaked. Please correct it.

## 5 · FUNCTION — W3 stopped contradicting its own tracker

W3 taught the three moves (Redirect / Reframe / Restart) and then said *"don't try to change anything yet. Just
notice."* The week's Playbook grid rows **are those three moves**, and the data field behind them records whether
she used them. The tracker was scoring the thing the copy told her not to do. Now:

> Here's your work this week: use it. When a false start happens, run the three steps. You're not building a plan
> yet — that comes later. Just catch them, and write down what you notice.

Greg's spec is intact — it forbids goal-setting and a structured change plan, and explicitly wants the member to
"react less automatically and recover more skillfully."

## 6 · FUNCTION — the Legacy Letter's text box defers

Chips only at the confirm; "Change a line" brings the composer up with its own prompt. Two testers wanted opposite
things on the same screen — one arrived to type, one arrived to accept — and deferring serves both.

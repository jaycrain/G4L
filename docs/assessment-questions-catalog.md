# PART 1 — ONBOARDING

## 1. The IDQ — Identity Distance Questionnaire

24 items, four dimensions (Physical / Self / Social / Outlook), 6 items each. **Scale 1–5**
(1 = *not landing at all* … 5 = *dead-on*). Scored into the ID Score (0–100). Schema, scoring,
and 60-day cadence are frozen.

### Physical
1. Your body feels like it belongs to you — you know it, you trust it, you're at home in it.
2. You could ask something hard of your body today without bracing for it to hurt.
3. You know your numbers — weight, blood pressure, resting heart rate — and you can look at them without looking away.
4. You're as physically capable today as you were five years ago.
5. The way you eat is something you actually decide — not something running on autopilot.
6. You sleep well, and you wake up with the day in front of you as something to show up for.

### Self
7. If your twenty-five-year-old self walked in right now, they'd recognize the person you've become.
8. You regularly make time for things you care about that have nothing to do with work or anyone else's needs.
9. Asked "who are you?", you've got an answer that isn't your job title or your role in the family.
10. You've held onto what you love — not quietly traded it away for something you don't.
11. The life you're in right now is one you chose, on purpose — eyes open.
12. You're the main character in your own story, not the supporting cast in everyone else's.

### Social
13. There's at least one person in your life who sees the real you and tells you the truth.
14. You show up all the way for the people you love — actually present, not just in the room.
15. You're the one who calls, who plans, who says yes — the one who shows up, not the one who bails.
16. Your closest relationships have gotten deeper this past year, not thinner.
17. You belong somewhere — a group, a community — really belong, not just attend.
18. When someone asks how you're doing, you tell them the truth.

### Outlook
19. You've got a clear picture of what you want your next chapter to be, past what you're obligated to do.
20. In the last six months you've set a goal that genuinely excites you — yours, not one handed to you.
21. You're willing to do hard things on purpose, because of who they're turning you into.
22. Picture the next ten years — you can see specific moments you want to be inside of.
23. In the last month you did something because the person you're becoming would do it.
24. You believe your best chapters are still ahead — and you're willing to bet on it.

## 5. B1 · "What Is Your Why?" — 12 SDT motivation items

**Scale 1–7** (1 = *Not at all true for me* … 7 = *Very true for me*). Two domains — physical
activity first (items 1–6), then healthy eating (7–12). Each domain: 3 autonomous, 2 controlled,
1 amotivation. Stored as a parallel motivation register; **not displayed in v2.4** (a lone snapshot
has no comparison — the scored profile waits for the quarterly re-read). Item stems are Greg's
verbatim science.

**Domain prompt (activity):** *Why do you want to be physically active regularly?*
1. I want to be physically active because it is personally important to me. *(autonomous)*
2. I want to be physically active because being active fits the kind of person I want to be. *(autonomous)*
3. I want to be physically active because I enjoy it or feel good when I do it. *(autonomous)*
4. I want to be physically active because I would feel guilty if I did not. *(controlled)*
5. I want to be physically active because other people expect me to. *(controlled)*
6. I do not really see why I should be physically active regularly. *(amotivation)*

**Domain prompt (diet):** *Why do you want to eat healthier?*
7. I want to eat healthier because it is personally important to me. *(autonomous)*
8. I want to eat healthier because it fits the kind of person I want to be. *(autonomous)*
9. I want to eat healthier because I enjoy taking care of my body. *(autonomous)*
10. I want to eat healthier because I would feel guilty if I didn't. *(controlled)*
11. I want to eat healthier because other people expect me to. *(controlled)*
12. I don't really know why I should eat healthier. *(amotivation)*

**Scale 1–5. FORMATIVE ONLY** — per Greg (RC-2) these are a reflective exercise: not scored, not

## 9. C2 · "The Bigger World Audit" — 20 items

A facilitated interview over the four IDQ domains. For each domain, five **1–10** ratings —
Current, Desired, Importance, Readiness, Ripple. 4 domains × 5 = 20 items. Greg's verbatim prompts.

### Physical — *your body: energy, movement, health habits, how you're treating yourself.*
- **Current** — When you think about your physical life right now — your energy, sleep, movement, health habits, and how you're treating your body — where would you rate yourself from 1 to 10?
- **Desired** — If 10 represents the physical version of you that feels strong, well-cared-for, and aligned with the life you want, where would you want to be?
- **Importance** — How important is it for you to close that physical gap right now, from 1 to 10?
- **Readiness** — How ready do you feel to work on this in the next 30 days, from 1 to 10?
- **Ripple** — If you made real progress here, how much would it improve other areas of your life — like mood, confidence, discipline, or relationships — from 1 to 10?

### Self — *who you're being: discipline, self-respect, steadiness, self-trust.*
- **Current** — When you think about who you are being right now — your discipline, self-respect, emotional steadiness, and the way you carry yourself — where would you rate yourself from 1 to 10?
- **Desired** — If 10 represents the version of you that feels grounded, self-trusting, and fully aligned with who you want to be, where would you want to be?
- **Importance** — How important is it for you to reduce that gap right now, from 1 to 10?
- **Readiness** — How ready are you to actively work on this in the next 30 days, from 1 to 10?
- **Ripple** — If you improved in this area, how much would it positively affect the rest of your life, from 1 to 10?

### Social — *your relationships: connection, belonging, presence with others.*
- **Current** — When you look at your relationships and social world right now — how connected, supported, honest, and present you feel with other people — where would you rate yourself from 1 to 10?
- **Desired** — If 10 represents the kind of relationships and social presence you want to have, where would you want to be?
- **Importance** — How important is it for you to work on this area right now, from 1 to 10?
- **Readiness** — How ready are you to do something about this in the next 30 days, from 1 to 10?
- **Ripple** — If this area improved, how much would it strengthen other parts of your life, from 1 to 10?

### Outlook — *your outlook: hope, direction, purpose, belief in what's possible.*
- **Current** — When you think about your outlook right now — your sense of hope, direction, purpose, and belief in what's possible — where would you rate yourself from 1 to 10?
- **Desired** — If 10 represents feeling clear, purposeful, forward-moving, and connected to a meaningful future, where would you want to be?
- **Importance** — How important is it for you to reduce that gap right now, from 1 to 10?
- **Readiness** — How ready do you feel to work on this in the next 30 days, from 1 to 10?
- **Ripple** — If this area improved, how much would it change other parts of your life, from 1 to 10?

*Scoring uses the **computed** gap (Desired − Current); the "how big does the gap feel" question is deliberately held as a reflective moment, not one of the 20 rated items.*

# APPENDIX A — Conversational probe sets (not rated)

These are structured *reflective* prompts, not assessment items — included so the catalog is

## Rewire W1 — "The Disinformation Audit" (the five domains)

The member is walked through five places their self-talk hides and names the story they actually
run. One question each (no rating).

1. **Your body** — What do you tell yourself about your weight, your energy, how you feel in your body day to day? ("I eat pretty healthy." "I'll clean it up when things settle." "It's just age.")
2. **Your habits** — What's the story that makes the extra drink, the skipped walk, the mindless eating after a hard day feel okay in the moment?
3. **Your time** — What do you tell yourself about why there's no room for you? ("I'm too busy." "When work calms down." "The kids need me.")
4. **Who you are** — What do you tell yourself about who you are now versus who you used to be? ("That was a long time ago." "I'm not that person anymore." "It's too late.")
5. **What's still possible** — What do you tell yourself about whether any of this can actually change? ("This is just who I am now." "It probably wouldn't work." "I've tried before.")

## Reconnect draw-out — *the Doors / the Drift / the Window*

Fully conversational (Motivational-Interviewing posture, one question at a time, member sets the
depth). No fixed wording — the Companion reflects and asks — so there is no verbatim item list to
catalog. Outputs: the member's **Door(s)**, the **Reclaim List**, and the baseline **ID Score**.

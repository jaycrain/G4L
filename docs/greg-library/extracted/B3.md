## Requirements

### [ID] B3-01
- **Type:** instrument
- **Requirement:** B3 is a two-part activity — a small lifestyle plan plus structured self-monitoring — run over the course of a week.
- **Verbatim:** "The Monitoring Health Decisions activity is designed to help a person learn from their everyday behavior by combining a small lifestyle plan with structured self-monitoring over the course of a week."
- **Source:** Science Check
- **Testable as:** The B3 asset delivers a planning phase AND a week-long tracking phase; neither alone completes the asset.

- **Source:** Science Check

## In-app summary (verbatim)

> This activity helps you learn from your everyday health decisions by combining a small weekly plan with daily self-monitoring. That matters because healthy change is easier to sustain when you can see what helps, what gets in the way, and what happens after a setback. This exercise asks you to try one small new physical activity habit and one small new dietary habit, then notice how those efforts actually unfold in real life. Along the way, you will track Smart Choices, False Starts, obstacles, thoughts, feelings, and how eating and movement influence one another. Research in this area suggests that self-monitoring can strengthen awareness, improve self-regulation, and make future change efforts more realistic. By doing this activity, you gain a clearer picture of how your lifestyle works, what supports better decisions, and what kinds of adjustments may help you stay on track over time.

## Contradictions

1. **Streaks as reward vs. streaks as mechanism.** The Companion Memo states the reward "is not a streak or a score — it is the felt sense of understanding one's own behavior more clearly," while both memos require streak tracking and streak affirmations at 3/5/7 days ("Track streaks; affirm consistency"; "Generate streak affirmations at milestones (3, 5, 7 days)"). Streaks are therefore permitted as a *tracked signal and affirmation trigger* but must not be presented to the member as the payoff. Resolvable, but easy to build wrong.

2. **"No running score" vs. streak display.** The tracker "must not display a running score, percentage, or compliance grade," yet UX 4 requires "Streak and pattern visibility — the Member can see their tracking rhythm." A visible streak count is a number; the distinction Greg intends is rhythm-of-tracking (allowed) vs. score-of-behavior (forbidden), but he does not say so explicitly.

3. **Check-in initiator is never stated.** The Companion Memo puts the Companion in the relatedness role with "daily or near-daily check-ins" and the Engineering Memo makes reminders *optional* and member-cued ("Reminder capability — optional check-in reminders"), while Autonomy requires "let the Member decide when to check in." Whether the Companion proactively initiates the daily check-in or waits for the member is left unresolved.

4. **Monitoring length.** The Science Check and Companion Memo say "a week" / "the monitoring week" flatly; the Engineering Memo softens it to "an ongoing monitoring period (nominally one week)" and streak milestones run to 7 days. Whether 7 days is a hard boundary or a default is not settled.

## Anything unclear

- **Who fires the daily check-in, and at what time.** See Contradiction 3. `check_in_cue` is captured but no memo says the system uses it to *initiate* a conversation vs. only to time an optional reminder.
- **What ends the week.** No memo defines the transition trigger from `monitoring` to `review` — day count, days_checked_in threshold, or member declaration. "The monitoring period has elapsed without engagement" implies an elapsed-time clock, but the value and the boundary behaviour (7 calendar days? 7 check-ins?) are unspecified.
- **What "quick-tap options for common patterns" contains.** Greg requires quick-tap tags but never enumerates a tag vocabulary for Smart Choices, False Starts, obstacles, or what_contributed. Someone has to author that list, and authoring it risks the system implicitly suggesting categories — which brushes against the autonomy constraint.
- **How `smart_choice_patterns` / `false_start_patterns` get populated.** Listed as outputs "(tagged or free text)" but no memo says whether these are member-authored at review, Companion-proposed and member-confirmed, or derived. Given the "no system verdict" rule, derivation looks disallowed, but pattern-surfacing is explicitly required ("surface patterns from the Member's own data to support their interpretation").
- **Mid-week revision and the tracker.** If a member revises `activity_habit` on day 4, no memo says whether prior days' entries are re-anchored, annotated, or left as-is against the old plan.
- **The relationship between the B3 monitoring habit and existing G4L commitments.** Greg does not address whether the two chosen habits become durable commitments after the week ends, or expire with the pilot. "Connect to what comes next (continued ReBuild work, B4 checkpoint, future cycles)" is the only forward hook.
- **Whether B3 can be re-run.** "Future cycles" is mentioned once; no memo specifies whether a member can start a second monitoring week with new habits, and what happens to the prior week's data if they do.

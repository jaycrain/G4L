### B2 scoring rules

VERBATIM
```
The self-management skills are in categories and the 4 point scale could then generate scores ranging from 1-4. Thus, for each category the scores would range from 2-8. A total score would range from 24 to 96; however, it would be more useful to create separate scores for diet and physical activity. The Self-Management Scores could be normalized to a percentages (so the numbers would be interpretable (PA: 32 out of 48 points would be 67%).

The scores could also be aggregated into the meta categories of self-management skills (Predisposing: 6,7,12 / Enabling: 1,3,4,5,8,11 / Reinforcing: 2,9,10). In this case, there would be 6 items making up Predisposing so scores would range from 6 to 24. A score of 18 would be a 75% value for self-management skills. The others can be similar.
```

Unpacked (mechanical, no interpretation added):

- Item scale 1–4. Each numbered skill ("category") = 2 items → **category range 2–8**.
- Total across all 24 items → **24–96**.
- Preferred: **separate PA and diet scores** — 12 items each → range 12–48. Normalize to a percentage
  (Greg's worked example: `PA: 32 out of 48 points would be 67%`).
- **Meta categories** (the three-way roll-up, Greg's exact grouping by skill number):
  - **Predisposing** = skills **6, 7, 12** (Balancing attitudes, Overcoming barriers, Building confidence and motivation)
  - **Enabling** = skills **1, 3, 4, 5, 8, 11** (Self-assessment, Goal Setting, Self-planning, Performance skills, Consumer skills, Time management)
  - **Reinforcing** = skills **2, 9, 10** (Self-monitoring, Social support, Relapse prevention)
  - Greg's worked example is stated for **Predisposing** as "6 items … range 6 to 24 … a score of 18 would be a 75% value". Note this counts 6 *items* for 3 skills — i.e. the meta-category is computed over **items** (3 skills × 2 items = 6 items, range 6–24). "The others can be similar": Enabling = 6 skills × 2 = 12 items, range 12–48; Reinforcing = 3 skills × 2 = 6 items, range 6–24.

The first step introduced you to core self-management skills that help with adopting and sustaining healthy lifestyles. To internalize these skills it is important to be able to recognize how they may help or hinder your behavior change efforts.

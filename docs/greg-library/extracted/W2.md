## Requirements

### [ID] W2-01
- **Type:** posture
- **Requirement:** W2 is a hybrid: the Companion is a guide for the front-end session and elevates to coach for the one-week daily practice.
- **Verbatim:** "The Companion's posture in W2 is a hybrid. The front-end session is guided and elicitive — the Companion helps the Member build a specific, sensory, emotionally vivid image rather than an abstract wish. The follow-on week of daily practice is more coach-like — the Companion checks in, helps the Member deepen the image, and notices when the disinformation campaign fires against it. W2 spans a single front-end session and a one-week daily practice, so the Companion must hold both postures in sequence."
- **Source:** Companion Memo
- **Testable as:** companion_mode reads `guide` for every turn of the front-end session and `coach` for every daily-practice turn; the switch is observable in the stored conversation metadata.

## In-app summary (verbatim)

> This activity helps you build a clearer picture of where you are going and who you are working to become. That matters because change gets much harder when the future stays vague. If you cannot see the destination clearly, it is easier to lose heart when results are slow or setbacks show up. This exercise helps you return to the spark you created earlier and strengthen it by making your future feel more real, personal, and worth pursuing. Research in this area suggests that people are more likely to stay committed when they can clearly imagine their future self and feel connected to that version of themselves. By doing this activity, you give your effort a clearer direction and a stronger reason to keep going.

## Contradictions

1. **"Five prompts" vs "seven-step sequence."** The Companion Memo's MI Phase 2 says the Companion helps build the image "through the five prompts (adjectives, moving through the world, an ordinary Tuesday, the conversation where the truth is good, the moment in the window)," while the same memo's sequence section and the Engineering Memo both specify a "seven-step visualization sequence." These reconcile (5 image prompts + identify-most-real + establish-practice = 7), but the copy is inconsistent and an implementer reading only Phase 2 would build five steps.

2. **Check-in cadence: "daily" vs "mid-week and end-of-week."** The Engineering Memo specifies "Daily / near-daily check-in" and "Phase 2: Daily practice (recurring check-ins)." The Companion Memo's Model conversation flow heading says "Daily check-ins (mid-week and end-of-week)" — i.e. two Companion contacts, not seven. The practice itself is daily in both; the number of *Companion* check-ins is not consistently stated.

3. **Streak tracking vs never-penalize.** The Engineering Memo requires `consecutive_streak` tracking and milestone affirmations at 3/5/7 days, while also requiring "Never penalize missed days" and "missed days are gaps, not failures." A visible consecutive streak that resets is itself a penalty signal; Greg does not say how the streak should render on a broken week.

## Anything unclear

1. **How the R3 Legacy Letter actually seeds W2.** Greg names the Legacy Letter twice — as prior module context to load on engagement, and as a cross-reference for "identity-related language" / "the Member's own words for the future self." He never specifies a mechanic: whether the Companion reads it back to the member at the opening, whether its phrases pre-populate anything, or whether it is only passive background for the model. There is no prompt in the seven-step sequence that references it.

2. **Who initiates the daily check-in.** The memos require check-ins and reminders but never say whether the Companion reaches out (proactive outreach) or the member opens the app and the check-in is waiting. This determines whether W2 needs the outreach system.

3. **What "practiced" means for logging.** The quick practice log asks "practiced?" but there is no definition of a qualifying rep — the script says five minutes, the affirmation says "even two minutes counts," and there is no minimum. Whether a self-reported tap is sufficient is unstated.

4. **What happens if the member never completes the front-end but the week elapses.** The graceful-suspend exit covers the practice week; there is no stated behavior for abandonment mid-sequence in the front-end session.

5. **Whether the image is revisable after the week closes.** `visualization_text` is described as "revisable" and image revision is a mid-week UX requirement, but Greg does not say whether the artifact freezes at the end-of-week review before it becomes W3's return-to point.

6. **Where the "one-week" clock starts.** Days are counted (days_elapsed, days_practiced) but Greg does not say whether day 1 is the day of the front-end session or the following morning, nor what happens if the member builds the image in the evening.

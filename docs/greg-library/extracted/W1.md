- **Type:** posture
- **Requirement:** The Companion runs W1 in guide mode, not full coach mode.
- **Verbatim:** "set companion\_mode = guide (lighter than full coach)" / "Because it is an awareness activity, the Companion's posture here is lighter than in W3 or B3 — more guide than coach."

## In-app summary (verbatim)

**In-app summary: W1 "Disinformation Audit"**

This activity helps you notice the stories you may be telling yourself about why change can wait, why things are not that serious, or why it might be too late. Those stories can feel reasonable in the moment, which is exactly why they are powerful. They can keep you stuck without sounding like excuses at all. This exercise helps you bring those patterns into the open and replace them with stronger, more honest statements about what is still possible. That matters because change gets easier when your mind is no longer quietly arguing against you. Research in this area suggests that the way you talk to yourself can shape motivation, resilience, and your willingness to stay engaged when change feels hard. By doing this activity, you start building an inner voice that supports effort instead of talking you out of it.

Also reproduced verbatim from the Companion Memo, the member-facing framing of the affirmation closure:

> The process of reflecting and tuning into mental traps and rationalizations will help you be more aware of barriers that you need to overcome. For each disinformation statement you drafted, create a more positive affirmation of what is possible. Tuning into your mind is critical for building Grinta.

## Contradictions

1. **Affirm for every statement vs. affirm only the most active ones.** The Science Check and the member-facing closure text say an affirmation is built for *each* statement — "For each disinformation statement you drafted, create a more positive affirmation of what is possible" and "generating a more constructive affirmation for each disinformation statement." The Engineering Memo scopes the builder to a subset — "Affirmation builder — guided closure that helps the Member construct and test credible affirmations for their **most active** statements" and "Repeat for the other active statements." The Companion Memo's Step 4 ("Let's do the same for the others") sits between the two. Unresolved: is closure complete when every statement has an affirmation, or when the active ones do?

2. **Companion Memo: prior module work = "ReConnect, including the IDQ, Fade Doors, and Legacy Letter"; the Engineering Memo loads all three (R1 IDQ, R2 Fade Doors, R3 Legacy Letter) but the cross-reference and personalization requirements only ever name R1 and R3.** R2 Fade Doors is loaded as context but has no specified use anywhere downstream in W1.

## Anything unclear

1. **Duration.** Neither memo states how long W1 takes or gives a turn budget — only that it is a single session with no monitoring period. There is no guidance on whether a suspended session that resumes the next day counts as the same session.
2. **What counts as "too generic."** The engine must "ask for one more layer of specificity" when a statement "is too generic," but no criterion or example threshold is given for the generic/specific judgement — and the docs simultaneously forbid grading statements.
3. **Silence window.** "Member has not responded within a reasonable window" is not quantified, and the same ladder is specified for a conversational surface where a member may simply be typing.
4. **"Signals readiness to act."** The elevation to coach mode is triggered by a member signal that is never defined, and it conflicts in spirit with the hard rule not to push toward action planning in W1 — what the Companion may actually *do* in elevated coach mode inside W1 is unspecified.
5. **Jay's member-facing script.** All three docs reference "Jay's disinformation campaign" opening script as an existing authored artifact but none reproduce it; its text is not in these documents.
6. **Minimum completion bar.** Since a member may skip all five domains and closure works from statements they produced, it is unstated what happens if a member reaches closure with zero captured statements.
7. **Credibility check storage.** `credibility_check (member-confirmed)` is specified as a field but its type (boolean, member's own words, or number of adjust loops) is not.

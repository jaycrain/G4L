## Contradictions

**1. "12 skills" vs. the enumerated skill lists — the count does not reconcile.**
Both the Companion Memo and the Engineering Memo assert 12 skills, repeatedly and structurally ("The Member self-assesses across 12 self-management skills"; "12 skill ratings captured with category assignments"; "Member overwhelmed by 12 skills"). But **no doc contains a numbered 12-item list.** The only enumerations are prose, and they do not total 12:

- **Science Check enumeration = 16.** Predisposing: building knowledge, confidence, motivation, overcoming barriers, balancing attitudes (**5**). Enabling: self-assessment, goal setting, planning, coping, consumer awareness, performance skill, time management (**7**). Reinforcing: self-monitoring, social support, conflict resolution, relapse prevention (**4**).
- **Companion Memo enumeration = 14.** Same Predisposing 5 and Reinforcing 4, but Enabling is given as "self-assessment, goal setting, planning, coping, time management" (**5**) — **dropping "consumer awareness" and "performance skill."**

So the Companion Memo silently drops two Enabling skills relative to the Science Check, and even its shorter list is 14, not 12. The canonical 12-item roster is **not defined in any of these three documents** and must be obtained from Greg before B2 can be built to spec.

**2. Physical activity vs. diet — asserted as a design property, never operationalised.**
The Science Check requires that B2 "addresses both physical activity and healthy eating in a balanced way" and explicitly wants the assessment to surface *differences* between the two behaviours ("good at planning movement but poor at managing eating cues"). But the Engineering Memo's data model has **one rating per skill** (`{skill_id, skill_name, category, rating}`) with no behaviour dimension, and the three-factor scores are single aggregates. As specified, the data model **cannot** represent the activity-vs-eating difference the Science Check calls for. Either the roster is 12 skills × 2 behaviours = 24 ratings (which would also explain a "12 skills" count separate from a 24-item instrument), or the Science Check's cross-behaviour requirement is unimplementable. This needs Greg's ruling.

**3. MI phase names vs. engineering stage names.**
Companion Memo phases: engagement → focus → evocation → informing → consolidation. Engineering Memo stages: engagement → assessment support → evocation → didactic informing → consolidation. The Engineering Memo also sets `mi_phase_progression` to the Companion Memo's five names while separately specifying its own five stage names. Minor, but two parallel five-step vocabularies are tracked in the same metadata object; "focus" and "assessment support" are not obviously the same thing.

## Anything unclear

1. **The 12-skill roster itself is missing.** See Contradictions #1. This is the single largest gap — the instrument cannot be built without it. We need Greg's exact 12 skill names, their exact member-facing wording, and their category assignments.
2. **The rating scale is nowhere specified.** No doc states the scale type, its range, its direction, or its anchor labels. There is no Likert definition, no 1–5 / 1–7, no "strongly agree"/"not at all like me" anchors, and no per-item stem wording. `rating` is an untyped field in the data model.
3. **How the category scores are computed is not specified.** The Engineering Memo says "Aggregate score for Predisposing skills" but never says whether that is a sum, a mean, a percentage, or a weighted roll-up — and the categories have unequal item counts under every enumeration given, so sum-vs-mean materially changes cross-category comparability. Since the whole point is to compare categories, this is load-bearing.
4. **How the score is shown to the member is underspecified.** We have strong constraints on what it must *not* look like (not a score to improve, no norms, no verdicts, development map) but no positive spec — no bar/radar/band/label guidance, and no statement of whether the member sees raw numbers at all.
5. **There is no Phase II logging period in B2.** Nothing in any of the three docs describes a logging or self-monitoring period, its length, or its mechanics. B2 as specified is a single-sitting self-assessment. The daily-behaviour tracking is explicitly deferred to B3 ("the next activity, where you'll start tracking some daily behaviors"). If a Phase II logging period is expected for B2, it is not in these documents.
6. **"Separately for physical activity and diet" is not stated as a rating instruction anywhere.** The Science Check wants the distinction surfaced; no doc instructs that each skill be rated twice. See Contradictions #2.
7. **No revisit cadence.** `intended_for_revisit` is required and the baseline must be "retrievable in future cycles," but no doc says when B2 is re-taken, on what trigger, or how the comparison is presented.
8. **"Sufficient response" is undefined.** Stage management says advance "only when the Member has provided sufficient response" without a threshold — this needs a deterministic contract or it becomes a model judgement call.
9. **"Prolonged disengagement" is undefined.** The third exit condition has no timeout value or turn count.
10. **The `total_score` is required but "less emphasized"** — unclear whether the member ever sees it, and if so, where.
11. **Skill-level definitions are required on request** ("brief explanations available on request") but **no definition text is supplied** for any skill in any of the three docs.
12. **Interaction with the Grinta Index / ID Score is unaddressed.** B2 produces a scored profile with three sub-scores; no doc says how this relates to (or must be kept distinct from) the platform's existing measures.

### Text Script for Introducing Activity (VERBATIM IN FULL — member-facing)

```text VERBATIM
Jay ran a disinformation campaign on himself for eight years. The lies didn't sound like lies. They sounded like reason. "I'm alright." "It's not that bad." "I'll deal with it next month." The whole time, his body was screaming the truth — the weight, the hives, the blood markers, three doctor's warnings — and his brain kept overriding the signal with the same comfortable story.

This exercise will help you determine what type of excuses and lies you might be in the habit of using on yourself. We will introduce questions about a specific category or domains and want you to share the type of statements you might tell yourself (or have told yourself in the past). Just share what comes to mind and not what you think you should think or believe.

About your body: What do you tell yourself about your weight, your fitness, your energy? (Examples: "I eat pretty healthy." "I'll clean it up when things settle down." "It's just age.")

About your habits: What do you tell yourself about the patterns you know aren't working? The extra drink, the skipped walks, the mindless eating after a hard day. What is the story that makes those patterns feel acceptable?

About your time: What do you tell yourself about why there's no room for you? (Examples: "I'm too busy." "When work calms down." "After the holidays." "The kids need me.")

About your identity: What do you tell yourself about who you are now versus who you used to be? (Examples: "That was a long time ago." "I'm not that person anymore." "It's too late.")

About the future: What do you tell yourself about whether change is possible? (Examples: "This is just who I am now." "It probably wouldn't work." "I've tried before.")
```

#### Daily monitoring categories — the tracking schema (VERBATIM TABLE)

```text VERBATIM
Daily monitoring categories

| Item | Meaning |
| Good decision | a choice that supported health, focus, or alignment |
| False start / drift moment | a moment where the Member went off course, avoided, justified, or acted on autopilot |
| What happened before | trigger, mood, stress, cue, environment, fatigue, urge |
| What story was present | excuse, rationalization, internal voice, permission statement |
| What happened after | effect on mood, energy, focus, pride, guilt, calm, rest |
```

# G4L — Onboarding Staged Script Outline (v0.1)

**Date:** June 26, 2026 · for Jay, to shape the UX
**Purpose:** the onboarding flow restructured into **stages** so it still *feels* like one warm conversation, but the structure shapes capture deterministically for the Companion. Pairs with the CC plan handoff (`2026-06-26-option-a-staged-capture-plan.md`).

**How to read this:** the stages are the skeleton the engine enforces; the copy stays conversational so the member never feels a form. Two patterns run throughout:

- **The transition IS the confirmation.** Each stage ends with a warm reflection that confirms what was captured *and* invites correction, then advances — no "Confirm? Y/N."
- **Stage = capture context.** While a stage is open, the Companion is only capturing *that* stage's field. That's what kills the 37% reclaim-as-gap mis-file.

`[FIXED]` = verbatim engine-owned · `[AGENT]` = agent-led within the stage · *(capture: X)* = what the engine records.

---

## Stage 0 — Open (the gate)

`[FIXED]` AI disclosure, upfront and explicit: *"A quick note before we start: you'll be talking with your Companion — an AI built for this one thing, helping you find your way back to yourself. It remembers what you share, so you never repeat yourself, and you can stop any time."*

`[FIXED]` Primer: *"This is the foundation for everything that follows, so give it room — about 15–20 minutes, no wrong answers, nothing timed. Here's how it goes: first who you are, then how the gap opened, then what you want back. Then your first ID Score."*

*(This primer doubles as the stage map — it tells the member the three stages before they happen, which is the orientation Donna wanted.)*

---

## Stage 1 — Who you are  *(capture: identity)*

`[FIXED]` open: *"Let's start by taking a minute to think about who you were back when you felt most like yourself — not the job title, the version underneath."*

`[AGENT]` narrow gradually — reflect what they say → offer a couple of candidate words **from their own language** → invite them to choose or coin one. Frame it as a **handle, changeable**, never a verdict. *(capture: identity)*

**↳ Confirm-transition into Stage 2:**
`[AGENT]` *"So — the Athlete is who we're bringing back, the one who felt most like you. Did I get her right? … Good. Now let's look at how the gap opened."*
*(reflection = confirmation + correction opening + reframe, in one move)*

---

## Stage 2 — How the gap opened  *(capture: the gap story + Door[s])*

`[AGENT]` reframe in (carried by the transition above). Then, gently:
*"Somewhere, the distance started to open. Sometimes it's one clear thing — a loss, a diagnosis, a move, a job that swallowed you. More often it's slower. Tell me how it went for you."*

`[AGENT]` receive **the fade story** — the narrative of how the distance grew. *(capture: gap)*

**Lighter Door posture — ask once, receive, forecast, advance:**
`[AGENT]` let the **main instigator** surface in their words, plus whatever else comes naturally. Don't excavate. *(capture: Door[s], recognition-over-routing — their words, may be one or several, may be uncertain)*
`[FIXED-ish]` forecast: *"Most people find there's more than one thread here — we'll go deeper in the Doors session when you're ready. For now this is plenty."*

**↳ Confirm-transition into Stage 3:**
`[AGENT]` *"So the gap started when your dad got sick and the caregiving swallowed everything — that's the thread we'll keep pulling. Sound right? … Now, the good part: let's talk about what you want back."*

---

## Stage 3 — What you want back  *(capture: Reclaim List)*

`[AGENT]` frame it first: *"These become your Reclaim List — the concrete things you're coming back for, in your own words. The whole program points at this list. Three to start, more if they keep coming."*

`[AGENT]` capture items. *(capture: reclaim_items)*

**↳ Confirm:**
`[AGENT]` *"Here's what you want back: [list]. Anything missing before we look at where you're starting from?"*

## Close → existing seatbelt

→ The **confirmation card** (unchanged — the member sees everything captured and can hit "something's missing, keep talking" before anything saves). Then → IDQ *(note: IDQ may lift out of onboarding — separate decision, no impact here)* → Threshold Ceremony → Tour.

## Front-loader handling (woven in, not a separate step)

If a member volunteers out-of-stage content — e.g., names what they want back while still in Stage 1 — the Companion **parks it in the moment** and **re-surfaces it warmly at its stage**:
> *(member in Stage 1: "…I just want to be writing again and not feel like a guest in my own life")*
> `[AGENT]` Stage 1 stays on identity, but the Companion notes those as reclaim items. Later, in Stage 3: *"Earlier you said you want to be writing again, and to stop feeling like a guest in your own life — let's start your Reclaim List there."*

This is the bar in action (*never drop what they gave you*) **and** the single best trust moment in the flow — it proves the Companion remembers everything.

## The engine view (why the copy is shaped this way)

| Stage | Member feels | Engine captures | Kills |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Who you are | a warm conversation about their best self | identity | — |
| 2 · How it opened | being understood, not interrogated | gap story + Door(s) | the 37% reclaim-as-gap (reclaim can't land here) |
| 3 · What you want back | hope, momentum | reclaim items | late-Door drop (Doors confirmed before this opens) |

Each transition is a confirmed boundary — so the engine always knows what it's capturing, and the member only ever feels a conversation moving naturally forward.

## The before-number (your batch, for reference)

8 rita runs: 50% clean · **37% reclaim-fragment-as-gap** · 13% partial (dropped `aging_parents`). The two dominant failures are both **context/sequencing** problems, not random: a reclaim message floats into the gap slot because nothing says "we're not collecting wants now," and `aging_parents` drops because it surfaces *after* "there's more" with no confirmed stage to hold it. That's the lens for the design below.

## Exploration 1 — STAGE the conversation (the biggest lever)

Today identity + Doors + reclaim are one continuous stream, which is *why* the engine has to guess which message is which. Proposal: segment into **confirmed stages**, each ending with a definitive ask-back to the member, then a reframe + screen advance — e.g., identity → "Now let's explore how the gap opened" (gap/Doors) → reclaim.

- **Why it's structural:** the **stage tells the engine what the message is.** Reclaim-as-gap (the 37%) becomes *impossible* — you're not collecting wants in the gap stage. A Door stage that won't advance until Doors are confirmed protects the late-surfacing `aging_parents` (the 13%).
- It's the state-machine form of the doc's own principle — *model proposes, engine disposes.*
- Bonus: warm reframe pages also answer Donna's "I didn't know what was being asked / where to start."
- **Caveats:** keep it warm, not a form/wizard (execution). And it must still **catch front-loaders** who volunteer out-of-stage info — stage = *default* capture context, per-field tools catch the overflow; neither alone.
- **This may reshape A:** from free-conversation per-field tools (disentangled by heuristic) into **stage-scoped capture with confirmed transitions** — likely *simpler*, and it closes the 37% + 13% structurally.

## Exploration 2 — lighter Door posture in onboarding

Reframe onboarding's Door job from "excavate the complete set" to **"receive whatever surfaces, don't probe, then forecast the Reconnect Doors exercise."** Not a count cap (a member can name several) — a *posture/contract* change that drops the completeness pressure driving much of the multi-Door guard density. Needs a deterministic rule (*ask once, take what's offered, forecast, advance*), not "dig until enough." Pairs with staging: this is what the "how it happened" stage does.

## Exploration 3 — the design question to answer in the shape

**Should A be stage-scoped capture with confirmed transitions, rather than per-field tools layered onto the current single stream?** The failure data points at staged. Bring the shape addressing this explicitly.

## 3. The Playbook layout — write to this

This is what you asked for and didn't have this morning. **Five tabs.** Every one has a Session behind it; that
test is what collapsed nine sections into five.

| Tab | What a member sees | Built from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This week** | the live 7-day practice grid, the Companion beside it, and the plays worth calling now | whichever week is running — W2, W3, B2, B3 or C3 |
| **Plays** | cards grouped by what they're FOR, each with the *situation* that calls for it and "Run it again with your Companion →" | W1 true lines · W2 picture · W3 protocol · B3 pilot · C3 Quality Days |
| **Reads** | the scouting report — what tells you *which* play to call | B2's development map · B1's why · C2's bigger world |

**Three things that fell out, and the reasoning matters for the copy:**

- **"Why it works" is gone as a chapter.** The science belongs *inside* a Read, next to the thing it explains.
- **"Your tells" moved into Reads.** A tell is literally what tells you which play to call — that's a read.
- **"What lights you up" moved into Who you are.**

**"My Story" now lives here**, off the Dashboard. Two stored narratives — the identity read ("You are someone
who…", written once) and the arc the Companion re-weaves at every Session close — sit together for the first
time, as one thing: *this is me, and this is where I've got to.*

**Built as of today: Plays, Reads, Who you are, Journal.** "This week" comes when the practice weeks move off
Momentum, which happens in one step for all four.

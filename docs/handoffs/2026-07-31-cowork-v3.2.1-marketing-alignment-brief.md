## 1. NAMING — one retirement, and it is load-bearing

### "Connect" is retired. It is **the Community**.

Jay's decision, 2026-07-30. Everywhere a member or the market can see: **the Community**.

**No member ever saw "Connect."** Treat it as a name that never shipped — there is no transition to explain, no
"formerly known as", no legacy to honour. Anywhere it survives in Cowork's glossary, campaign drafts, or book
manuscript, it is simply wrong and should be replaced, not migrated.

**What changed in the app:** the member-facing screens already said "G4L Community"; what was actually wrong was
the **Member Agent's own vocabulary** — its instructions used "Connect" eight times, so the Companion would have
said a retired name out loud to a member. That is fixed, and the prompt now explicitly forbids the word.

**⚠️ DO NOT "correct" these — they are the verb, not the product name.** They appear in the transcript and read
correctly as-is:
- "Connect Strava and your rides, runs, and workouts flow in on their own."
- "Connect apps to automatically record activity."
- "Connect with other members →"  *(this is the CTA INTO the Community — a call to action, not a label)*

The rule: **"the Community" is the place. "connect" is something a member does.**

### Carried forward from the last brief (unchanged, restating for safety)
- **Comeback** = the outcome — you've reclaimed your identity and your goals. The elevated hero term
  ("Start my comeback", "10,000 comebacks").
- **"journey"** is now just an ordinary word, usable in prose. It is no longer a locked capitalised feature.
  Do **not** mechanically swap Journey → Comeback; they mean different things.

## 2. STORY — how the member's own words get captured changed

This is the most quotable shift in v3.2.1, and it strengthens a claim marketing can now make honestly.

### The Reclaim List is now written BY THE MEMBER, verbatim.

Previously the Companion inferred the list from conversation. That lost roughly **30%** of what people said. Since
2026-07-29 the member types each item into a **list builder**, and those exact entries *are* the list — stored
word for word, never paraphrased, never merged, never "tidied".

**Why it matters to the story:** "your list, in your words" is now literally true rather than aspirational. The
app does not summarise a member's wants back at them and ask them to accept its version. Members write it; we keep
it exactly.

**Also removed:** the Companion used to reconcile near-duplicate or multi-part entries ("these two sound like the
same thing — merge them?"). That interrogation of a member's own typed words is **gone**. If someone writes three
wants in one line, that is their line to keep.

### The identity word is now chosen, not assigned.

The member is offered candidate words drawn from their own language as tappable chips, plus a
**"write your own"** field. Their pick is captured verbatim and is definitive. The Companion never names an
identity the member didn't choose. (This enforces the governance rule: *never names an identity label without
member confirmation*.)

### The Companion stays in its lane during the "how it happened" conversation.

It no longer assembles a Reclaim List mid-story. It reflects the member's **story** back — how the distance
opened — and leaves the list to the step the member controls.

## 3. NEW MEMBER-FACING SURFACES (new copy exists — worth knowing it's there)

**Account recovery, which did not exist before v3.2.1:**
- `/login` now carries **"Forgot your password?"**
- `/login/forgot` — "Reset your password" · *"Tell us the email you signed up with and we'll send you a link."* ·
  button **"Send me a reset link"**
- `/login/reset` — "Choose a new password" · **"Set my password"**
- `/login/verify` — "Confirm your email"
- Two transactional emails: a password-reset link and a "Confirm your email" welcome link.

**Deliberate copy decision worth mirroring in any support material:** the reset request says the *same thing*
whether or not the address is registered — *"If that address has an account, we just sent a link…"* Never write
marketing or help copy that implies we'll confirm whether someone is a member. Membership here is itself private.

## 4. FUNCTION — claims that are now TRUE that weren't

Useful for trust/privacy messaging. All shipped and verified in v3.2.1:

- **Account recovery exists.** A member who forgets their password can get back into their own story.
- **Pseudonymity holds.** A member's real name can no longer surface as an actor label in the Community when they
  cheer before ever posting. Their handle is theirs.
- **Crisis routing covers every member-authored field** — post bodies, post *titles*, and live-room titles. It was
  previously body-only.
- **Proactive nudges honour the member's own settings** — their stated rhythm, their quiet hours, and an explicit
  opt-out. Nothing lands at 3am; "only when I ask" is absolute.
- **Session security hardened** — tokens hashed at rest, failed-login throttling, sessions revoked on password
  change, standard security headers.

**Tone caution:** these are *table stakes done properly*, not differentiators to trumpet. The honest register is
"of course" — not "look what we built." Overclaiming security invites scrutiny of everything we haven't done yet.

# The Threshold — ceremony copy (for wordsmithing)

This is the spoken script of the Threshold ceremony, beat by beat. **Edit the wording freely here**,
then hand it back and I'll drop it into the one config block (`THRESHOLD_COPY` in
`lib/ceremony/threshold-beats.ts`) — they mirror each other 1:1.

**What's load-bearing (please keep):** the *number and order* of beats, that Beat 7 is the clip-in
hand-off, and the `{…}` data slots (those render the member's own values — don't turn them into prose).
Everything else is yours to shape. Delivery is a typewriter reveal, one beat at a time, member-advanced;
all text renders in regular weight (no bold).

---

> Your Playbook fills in with what works for you as we go. That's the thing you keep — it's how the Grinta lasts.
